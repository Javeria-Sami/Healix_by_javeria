# 02 — Business Requirements

## Primary Goals
1. Explain what Healix is.
2. Explain services.
3. Establish trust.
4. Demonstrate previous work/capability.
5. Make conversion easy.

## Audiences
- Patients/general visitors
- Healthcare professionals
- Healthcare organizations
- Business decision makers

## Conversion
Primary CTA should be selected from the real business model: Book Consultation, Get Started, Contact Healix, Request a Service, or Request a Quote.

## Acceptance
The visitor should be able to understand the organization, evaluate its services/capability, and take the next step without confusion.
