# 07 — Content Strategy

## Brand Voice
Warm, clear, confident, human, responsible.

## Avoid
- Empty corporate buzzwords.
- Generic AI slogans.
- Unsupported medical promises.
- Fake statistics.
- Fake testimonials.

## CTA Language
Prefer specific actions:
- Explore Services
- View Case Study
- Talk to Healix
- Book a Consultation
- Get Started

## Content Hierarchy
Eyebrow → Heading → Supporting statement → Details → Action.

## Healthcare Accuracy
All medical information, professional credentials, statistics, and claims require client verification.
