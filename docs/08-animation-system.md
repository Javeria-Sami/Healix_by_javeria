# 08 — Animation System

## Philosophy
Motion should explain, guide, or delight. Remove animation that does none of these.

## Levels
1. Micro motion — buttons/icons/links.
2. Component motion — cards/menus/accordions.
3. Section motion — hero and scroll reveals.
4. Showcase motion — selective 3D.

## Suggested Timing
Fast: 150–200ms
Normal: 250–400ms
Slow: 500–800ms

## Effects
Fade, slide, scale, stagger, mask reveal, subtle parallax, counters.

## Reduced Motion
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Disable or simplify large transforms, parallax, and decorative animation.
