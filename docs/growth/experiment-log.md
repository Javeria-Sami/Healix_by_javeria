# Healix Healthcare — Controlled Experimentation Log

## 1. Ethical Healthcare Experimentation Standards
- **Zero Dark Patterns**: Deceptive countdown timers, artificial urgency badges, and hidden charges are strictly forbidden.
- **Privacy & Compliance**: Experiments must never alter clinical accuracy, HIPAA warnings, or medical disclaimers.
- **Accessibility Invariance**: All test variants must maintain WCAG 2.1 AA compliance and visible focus indicators.
